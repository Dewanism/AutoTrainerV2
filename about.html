Why I started this

Good people. Good dealerships.

I've been in the car business over 30 years, first selling, then managing. The number I'm proudest of isn't an award. It's 465. That's how many people I've trained who went on to become top sellers at their dealerships.

I started The Auto Trainer 14 years ago because I kept seeing the same thing. Good people couldn't get in the door without experience, and dealerships kept hiring and losing staff and starting over. Same fix for both: train people properly, and put them in the right store.

Since then I've trained over 2,000 candidates and worked with more than 60 dealerships around the Lower Mainland.

No sales experience needed. I'll train you, and I'll teach you things you won't pick up on your own. How to build rapport. How to find common ground. A few tricks that took me years to learn. When you're ready, I'll work on getting you in front of a dealership for an interview.

After that it's on you. What you do with the training is what decides how you do. Most of my graduates make $40,000 to $50,000 their first year, and plenty clear six figures by year four. There's no ceiling in this business.

Good people. Good dealerships. That's it.
