The Auto Trainer Program

Learn to sell cars from Paul

Training, placement help, and a mentor who stays in your corner after you hit the floor.

Learn the Fundamentals

The Auto Trainer program walks you through the full road to the sale: the meet and greet, the interview, car selection, the test drive, and the close. You will learn the process that every successful salesperson runs, step by step.

Get Started

Practice Before You Sell

Knowing the steps is not the same as running them under pressure. You will role play real customer conversations and the objections that come with them, so your first day on the floor is not your first time saying the words out loud.

More About Training

Interview and Resume Prep

Getting hired is its own skill. We help you put together a resume that a sales manager will actually read, and we prepare you for the questions you will get asked so you walk into that interview ready.

Get Started

Dealership Placement

Through Paul's dealership connections across Vancouver, he will work to help you secure a placement. Please note this does not guarantee a job. What happens in the interview room is up to you, but you will not be walking in cold.

More About Placement

Mentorship That Continues

Training does not end on your first day at the dealership. When you hit a tough deal, a difficult customer, or a slow month, Paul is a phone call away. Advice and support stay available long after placement.

More About Mentorship

Taught by Someone Who Sells

This is not classroom theory from a textbook. Paul has spent 30 years on the floor and in management, and he teaches the same approach he uses himself. You learn what works today, not what worked a decade ago.
